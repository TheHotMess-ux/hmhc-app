🖤 HMHC Founding Principles

• One brick at a time.

• Never ask a question the app already knows.

• Reduce mental load before adding features.

• Validation before education.

• Real women. Real experiences. No perfection required.

• Make every interaction feel like checking in with a friend.

• Every tap should have a purpose.

• Build for the woman who is exhausted, overwhelmed, and trying her best.
