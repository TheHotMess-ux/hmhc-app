# HMHC Mission

We believe women deserve better.

Better education.

Better tools.

Better support.

Better answers.

The Hot Mess Hormone Club exists to help women understand their bodies, advocate for themselves, and feel less alone through every stage of hormonal change.

Every feature we build should answer one question:

"Does this genuinely make someone's life a little easier today?"

If the answer is yes...

We're building the right thing.
